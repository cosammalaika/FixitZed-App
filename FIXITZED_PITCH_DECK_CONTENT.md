## Slide 2

**Background**

FixItZed is building a trusted digital layer for Zambia’s home-services market, starting in Lusaka. The market is large, fragmented, and still mostly coordinated through informal referrals, WhatsApp messages, Facebook groups, and direct calls to technicians. That creates friction for both households and service professionals.

Zambia is also becoming more digitally addressable:
- ZamStats reported `4,087,031` households in Zambia in the 2022 Census, including `1,917,552` urban households.
- Lusaka Province had `3,093,617` people in 2022, the largest provincial population in the country.
- GSMA reported that `76.1%` of the urban population aged 10+ had used a mobile phone in the prior 3 months in 2022.
- GSMA also reported `12.5 million` mobile internet subscriptions in Zambia by the end of 2023.

**Problem**

The customer problem:
- It is hard to find reliable plumbers, electricians, cleaners, and handymen quickly.
- Customers do not know who to trust, what the real price should be, or whether the fixer will actually arrive.
- Most of the process is offline, so there is poor visibility, weak accountability, and limited records for repeat use.
- Cash handling, quote disputes, and no-show risk increase customer anxiety.

The fixer problem:
- Good professionals struggle to stand out in an unstructured market.
- They rely too heavily on word of mouth.
- They need a repeatable source of jobs, ratings, and customer trust.

Target personas:
- Busy urban households
- Families managing recurring repairs and maintenance
- Landlords and rental portfolio owners
- Estates, apartments, and retail/commercial property managers
- Skilled fixers looking for steady, higher-trust demand

What currently solves the problem:
- Informal referrals from friends and family
- WhatsApp and Facebook groups
- Direct calls to local technicians
- Local service marketplaces such as Yogig
- Residential/community platforms such as TownConnect

## Slide 3

**Solution**

FixItZed is a mobile-first home-services marketplace and concierge platform that helps households and property managers find vetted fixers, book jobs, track service delivery, and pay securely in one flow.

What the customer gets:
- Fast access to trusted electricians, plumbers, cleaners, handymen, and renovation specialists
- A simpler booking flow with clearer communication
- Job tracking, ETA visibility, and approvals inside the app
- Secure digital payments and digital records after every visit
- A repeatable way to rebook trusted professionals

What the platform already communicates publicly:
- Customers can book trusted fixers from the app
- They can approve quotes, track arrivals, and pay securely
- They can plan recurring maintenance, share images, and manage work from the app
- They can save favorite fixers and unlock loyalty rewards

Core service suites already visible on the public site:
- Electrical and smart home
- Plumbing and water care
- Cleaning and housekeeping
- Renovation and finishing
- Security and related home support

## Slide 4

**How it Works**

1. Share your request

The customer submits a request in the app, chooses a service, picks a time, and provides location details and notes.

2. Match with a fixer

FixItZed matches the request with a vetted specialist based on skill, availability, and ratings.

3. Track progress live

The customer receives updates, tracks arrival status, reviews milestones, and stays informed during the job.

4. Rate and pay

The customer pays in-app, stores digital records, rates the fixer, and can save them for future work.

**Serviceable Available Market (SAM)**

Reachable near-term market:

Practical SAM statement:

`FixItZed’s SAM is urban households, estates, apartments, and commercial property managers in Zambia, starting with Lusaka and expanding into other dense urban corridors.`

**Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**

Immediate beachhead:
- FixItZed already publicly claims `800+ households in Lusaka`
- The first obtainable market is the subset of Lusaka households and property managers that repeatedly need trusted, cashless, on-demand home services

Practical SOM statement:

`FixItZed’s initial SOM is the repeat-use urban household and property-management segment in Lusaka, where the company already has visible traction.`

Demand-side proof:

## Slide 9

**Competition**

1. Informal market

Friends, referrals, Facebook groups, WhatsApp, and direct technician calls still dominate the market.

Strengths:
- Familiar
- No onboarding friction

Weaknesses:
- Low trust
- No standard vetting
- No ETA visibility
- Weak accountability
- Mostly cash-based and poorly documented

2. Yogig

Yogig presents itself as a Zambia service marketplace for home and personal services, including plumbing, electrical, cleaning, beauty, and more.

Strengths:
- Broad service marketplace
- Booking and verified provider positioning

Weaknesses versus FixItZed:
- Less visibly focused on the premium home-maintenance and property-management use case
- Less differentiated concierge positioning

3. TownConnect

TownConnect positions itself as a residential and society-management platform with home-services features.

Strengths:
- Strong gated-community and society-management angle
- Home services built into a broader residential system

Weaknesses versus FixItZed:
- Broader platform focus
- Less clearly positioned as a standalone trusted home-services brand for the wider urban household market

**How we stand out**

- Stronger trust narrative: vetted fixers, ratings, and quality control
- Better customer visibility: tracking, approvals, job notes, and notifications
- Better payment experience: cashless billing and digital invoices
- Better retention mechanics: favorites, loyalty rewards, referrals, and repeat booking
- Better positioning: a focused home-services and maintenance brand, not a general marketplace
- Better B2C plus B2B2C path: households plus estates, apartments, and property managers

Suggested 2x2 axis for the slide:
- X-axis: `Low trust` to `High trust`
- Y-axis: `Offline / informal experience` to `Digitally managed experience`

Place FixItZed in the `High trust + Digitally managed` quadrant.
